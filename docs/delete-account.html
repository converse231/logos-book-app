Delete your account

Quire — a reading tracker, by the Quire developer.

Last updated 11 August 2026

You can permanently delete your Quire account and all of its data at any time, either from inside the app or by email if you no longer have it installed.

Option 1 — From inside the app

  1. Open Quire and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm on the prompt that appears.

Deletion happens immediately — there is no waiting period.

Option 2 — By email

If you can't access the app, email danielparagas5@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm your identity against that address and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — email, display name, username, bio, birth year, profile photo
  • Your library — every book on your shelves, status, and progress
  • Your reading sessions — pages, minutes, timestamps, and history
  • Your streak, XP, level, and unlocked achievements
  • Your reviews and ratings
  • Your reading goals and generated insights
  • Your notification settings and push token
  • Anyone you follow or who follows you

This is permanent and cannot be undone. Consider using Settings → Account → Export my data first if you'd like a copy.

Delete some of your data, without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove specific data. Two things can be deleted individually, from inside the app, at any time:

  • A single reading session — open the session from Stats → History (or from Home) and tap Delete. This removes that session's pages, minutes, and timestamps, and reverses the XP it earned. Your streak and badges are not affected.
  • A book on your shelf — open the book and tap Remove from library. This deletes every reading session logged against that book. Your XP, level, and streak are not affected.

If you'd like something deleted that isn't covered by either control above — for example a specific review — email danielparagas5@gmail.com describing what you'd like removed, from the email address on your account, and we'll complete it within 7 days.

What's retained, and why

Feedback and bug reports you submitted through the app are kept, but disconnected from your identity — they're no longer linked to your account or email in any way. We keep these to improve the app; deleting your account does not delete this anonymised record.

Nothing else is retained. There is no extended backup-retention period beyond what's needed to complete the deletion itself.
